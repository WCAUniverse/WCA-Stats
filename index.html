Join the Roster

Apply to become an official WCA wrestler. Sign-ups are reviewed personally — submit a serious pro-wrestling character (representing you or fully original) and it gets created or imported into WWE 2K25, balanced to a hard cap of 85 overall, then added to the roster.

Taken seriously. Characters must read as legit pro wrestlers. Influences are welcome with a clear wrestling spin — no joke gimmicks.
85 OVR cap. Every wrestler is balanced to 85 overall or lower. The detail you give helps keep that fair.
How it works. You apply → I review → I create/import & balance the CAW → you debut on the roster.
WCA runs on PC. WWE 2K25/2K26 has no full cross-platform sharing — a CAW made on Xbox Series X|S or PS5 can't be imported to PC. So if your existing character is on a current-gen console, your share code won't work for us. No problem: just fill out the application as a new creation with as much detail as you can, and I'll rebuild it from scratch on PC. Only CAWs already on PC can be imported directly.
